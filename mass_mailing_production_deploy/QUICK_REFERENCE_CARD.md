# 🚀 Candidate CRM - Quick Reference Card

## 🌐 Access
**URL**: http://localhost:5173/pipeline-crm

---

## ⚡ Quick Actions

### 👤 Add New Candidate
1. Click **"Add Candidate"** button
2. Fill form: Name, Email, Stage, Priority, Recruiter
3. Click **"Create"**

### 🔄 Move Candidate
**Drag & Drop**: Click and drag candidate card to new stage
**Dropdown**: Click candidate → "Move to..." → Select stage

### 📝 Add Note
1. Click candidate card
2. Go to **"Notes"** tab
3. Click **"Add Note"**
4. Enter content and type
5. Click **"Save"**

### 🏷️ Add Tag
1. Click candidate card
2. Go to **"Tags"** tab
3. Click **"Add Tag"**
4. Enter name and color
5. Click **"Save"**

---

## 🎨 Pipeline Stages
- **Applied** → **Screening** → **Interview** → **Offer** → **Hired**
- **Rejected** (can be from any stage)

## 🏷️ Priority Levels
- **Low** (Gray) - Standard
- **Medium** (Blue) - Normal
- **High** (Yellow) - Important
- **Urgent** (Red) - Critical

## 📝 Note Types
- **General** - General notes
- **Interview** - Interview feedback
- **Feedback** - Performance feedback
- **Follow-up** - Action items

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### White Screen?
- Hard refresh: **Ctrl+F5** (Windows) or **Cmd+Shift+R** (Mac)

### Drag & Drop Not Working?
- Use dropdown method instead
- Check browser compatibility

### Data Not Saving?
- Check browser console (F12)
- Verify backend: http://localhost:8809/health
